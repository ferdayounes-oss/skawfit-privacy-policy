Privacy Policy for SkawFit

Effective Date: September 14, 2026 • Developer: Younes AITNASSAR

At SkawFit (Package Name: com.skaw.skawfit), we place the highest priority on protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy document transparently outlines the nature of the data we collect, how we use it, its security scope, and your available choices regarding your data when using our application on Android.

1. Identity Information and Account Data

To enable you to maximize tracking and personalization features, we collect the following data:

  • Personal Account Data: Name, email address, and profile picture when using Google Sign-In or Firebase Auth authentication.
  • Physical and Fitness Profile: Age, gender, height, current and target weight, physical activity level, adherence rate, body injuries (with manually entered additional details), and diet or food allergy profiles.
  • Social Performance Data: Unique user ID (SkawFit ID), display name, fitness scores (BodyScore), and daily streak (Streak) visible to the digital community inside the app.

2. Android Health Connect Integration & Health Data

The application integrates with the Android Health Connect framework to read and sync vital health metrics (such as body weight, steps, calories burned, and heart rate) to monitor your progress and dynamically update your workout and nutrition plans.

  • We only read this data based on your explicit and direct permission.
  • Health Connect data is never sold, rented, or shared with any third parties for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage purposes.

3. Camera Processing and Artificial Intelligence (Gemini)

  • Camera for Posture Analysis: The camera is used locally on your device exclusively to process and analyze body posture. No video frames or personal images are ever uploaded or stored on cloud servers.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Only selected measurements and text data are sent to the artificial intelligence model (Gemini via Cloud Functions) to generate customized plans, without attaching your email address or explicit personal identity.

4. Third-Party Services and Technologies Used

The application relies on certified infrastructure packages and services to ensure operational stability, including:

  • Google Firebase: For database management (Firestore), cloud storage, authentication, and system notifications (FCM).
  • Google AdMob: To serve relevant ads with full compliance with European and local user consent frameworks (UMP) utilizing the Advertising ID.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Firelog: To collect technical diagnostic crash reports completely isolated from your identity or sensitive health data.
  • Open-Meteo API: To retrieve local weather data based on coarse location to improve outdoor workout experiences.

5. Data Security and Protection

We adopt advanced engineering security standards including encrypted secure storage (SecureStorage), strict encrypted communication protocols (HTTPS), and system-level blocks against unauthorized data backups to ensure the confidentiality of your information.

6. Children's Privacy

SkawFit does not target individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from this age group. If any data related to a child is discovered, it is deleted immediately.
