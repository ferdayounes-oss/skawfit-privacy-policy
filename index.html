1. Information We Collect

SkawFit may process information you provide, information generated through your use of the app, and technical information required to operate, secure, and improve the service.

Account and Identity Information

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo
  • SkawFit user ID
  • Authentication information
  • Account timestamps and settings

This information supports authentication, account management, synchronization, friends, challenges, leaderboards, and program sharing.

2. Health & Fitness Information

Depending on the features you choose to use, SkawFit may process information such as:

  • Age
  • Height and weight
  • Target weight
  • Activity level
  • Fitness goals
  • Training experience
  • Workout history
  • Exercise performance
  • Fitness progress
  • Body measurements and body-analysis information
  • Nutrition and calorie information
  • Food preferences and dietary restrictions
  • Recovery-related information
  • Workout and nutrition plans
  • Saved fitness programs
  • User-created workout and nutrition programs

This information is used to provide workout plans, nutrition plans, progress tracking, personalization, fitness analysis, and related app functionality.

SkawFit does not sell health or fitness information or use it for advertising profiling.

Android Health Connect

When you explicitly grant permission, SkawFit may access supported Android Health Connect information, which may include:

  • Steps
  • Active energy
  • Sleep information
  • Heart rate
  • Resting heart rate
  • Weight
  • Other supported fitness metrics

Health Connect information is used for fitness tracking, recovery insights, progress analysis, and personalization.

3. Location Information

Precise Location

When you use location-based weather functionality and grant location permission, SkawFit may obtain your device's latitude and longitude.

This information may be transmitted to the Open-Meteo weather API to provide weather information relevant to your location.

Approximate Location

SkawFit may also process approximate location information for features where a less precise location is sufficient, including certain regional or community-related functionality.

SkawFit does not use precise location to continuously track your movements in the background.

You can manage location permissions through Android system settings.

4. Camera & Speech Features

Camera

SkawFit may request camera access for body-posture or body-analysis functionality.

Where applicable, processing is performed on the device. SkawFit does not intentionally upload or permanently store raw camera video for posture analysis.

Speech

Some SkawFit features may use speech recognition. Microphone access is requested only when the relevant feature is used.

Where supported by the device and operating system, speech recognition is performed on-device. SkawFit does not intentionally store raw microphone recordings as user content.

5. Artificial Intelligence Features

SkawFit may use artificial intelligence to assist with personalized fitness and nutrition plan generation.

When cloud processing is required, selected information relevant to the requested feature may be transmitted to SkawFit's backend infrastructure and/or an AI processing provider.

This may include fitness goals, training preferences, nutrition-related information, and other information necessary to generate the requested result.

We aim to minimize unnecessary personal information sent for AI processing. Where technically available, SkawFit may use local or deterministic processing instead of cloud AI processing.

AI-generated content is provided as fitness and wellness assistance and is not medical diagnosis or medical advice.

6. User-Created Programs & Social Features

SkawFit allows users to create workout, nutrition, and combined fitness programs.

Depending on the available features, users may also participate in:

  • Friends
  • Challenges
  • Leaderboards
  • Program sharing
  • Fitness scores
  • Streaks and progress features

When you voluntarily use these features, information necessary for the selected feature may become visible to participating SkawFit users.

For example, a shared fitness program may contain the information necessary for another user to view or import that program.

7. Third-Party Services

SkawFit uses technology providers that help operate the application.

Depending on the feature, these services may include:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, databases, cloud functions, storage, and notifications.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting, diagnostics, and application performance information.
  • Google AdMob — advertising and advertising-related services.
  • Open-Meteo — weather information based on location required for the weather feature.
  • Google AI / Gemini infrastructure — selected cloud AI processing when required by an AI-powered feature.

These providers may process information according to their own terms, privacy policies, technical requirements, and applicable law.

SkawFit does not sell personal information.

8. Advertising, Analytics & Diagnostics

SkawFit may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertising-related technologies may process information such as advertising identifiers, device or app identifiers, basic device information, and ad interaction information.

Where required, SkawFit uses applicable consent mechanisms to manage advertising and privacy choices.

SkawFit may also use Firebase Crashlytics and related technical services to understand crashes, errors, application performance, and stability issues.

Technical information may include crash logs, diagnostic information, application performance data, device and operating-system information, application version information, and technical identifiers.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Depending on the information and service, these measures may include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted communications
  • Firebase security controls
  • Authentication and authorization mechanisms
  • Secure local storage
  • Access controls
  • Server-side validation
  • Database security rules

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. We continuously review and improve SkawFit's security.

10. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested functionality, maintain the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the security of the service.

Account Data

Account and profile information is generally retained while your account remains active, unless deletion or another applicable legal requirement requires earlier removal.

Fitness and App Data

Workout history, nutrition information, saved plans, manually created programs, and related fitness information are retained to provide progress tracking and personalization while your account remains active.

Technical Data

Crash, diagnostic, security, and performance information may be retained according to the retention practices of the relevant technical service providers.

Legal and Security Retention

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required by law, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal obligations.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Accessing information associated with your account
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion
  • Withdrawing permissions

13. Children's Privacy

SkawFit is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

If a different minimum age is required by applicable law in your jurisdiction, the applicable legal requirement will apply.

14. International Data Processing

SkawFit uses cloud and technology providers that may process information on servers located in countries other than your country of residence.

Where applicable, appropriate safeguards and contractual or technical measures are used for international data transfers.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SkawFit's features, technology, legal requirements, or data practices change.

When material changes are made, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the application.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.
